GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6


Nothing but harsh howl of the tornado's winds and the warm, cozy music emanating from the food truck echoed through the ruined town. Over the devastated landscape, the air, carried by the music.

It was then. A vivid red light burst through the gaps in the tornado. What had begun as a faint glow steadily intensified until it broke through the swirling storm and surged upward. The tornado began to shrink in size as if losing its power, and the giant whirlwind gradually dwindled into a small vortex. As the gaps in the vortex dissolved, heavy objects came crashing down to the ground. The sandstorm slowly settled, and through the haze, the members could be seen hugging each other tightly. They held onto one another with desperate urgency, each saving the other, bound together so firmly that they almost resembled a single egg. Once the last grains of sand had finally fallen to the floor, the members were also returned to the ground. Like flower petals slowly blooming, one by one, thet separated from each other. Tears welled in their eyes as the relief of knowing everyone was safe washed over them. At the center of the fully bloomed "flower" was Woo Young, carefully cradling the ruby-colored light like a mother bird caring for their young. Resting in Woo Young's hands was Sopro. It had returned to the same size it was when the first discoverd it in World Z. Still on his knees, Woo Young spoke soft, gentle voice toward Sopro resting on his palm, as if making a heartfelt confession.

Woo Young
"I felt like I was alone, too. I was resentfull and angry. I wanted evryone to feel the same way I did. Failing to achieve my dream, then finding something to replace it and convincing myself I was satisfied with that... it felt like living a life of failure."

The members wuietly went and stood by Woo Young's side. No one wanted to interrupt him.

Woo Young
"But I finally understand now. We don't live by the outcomes. We live by the memories of what we experienced. Failing to achieve a dream doesn't mean your life is over. Life is so much bigger than any dream."

The Woo Young of the past had been so quick to believe that achievements and dreams were all there was to life. That the only way to prove his worth was to make his dream come true. And if he failed to achieve it, then he would remain someone who had never proven himself. That if he failed, he was nothing more than a worthless existence. 'Why was I so desperate to prove myself to the world?' Woo Young thought to himself. 'When my existence itself is what proved me.'

Woo Young
"I always new the truth deep down. That there are more people who live without achieving their dreams than those who do achieve them. And I knew that all those people's lives weren't failures either. But, for some reason, I just couldn't apply that same truth to myself. I was foolish."

Sopro shimmered with a ruby glow, as if it were listening to Woo Young's words.

Woo Young
"Life isn't just some straight line heading toward a single destination point. What matters more is sharing meals and spreading love with the people we cherish. The strength that keeps me going is actually just those small, precious memories."

Woo Young
"Like a mother birst bringing their chick into the world, I brought you out, but I'm sorry I couldn't teach you about these precious, warm feelings first. Even if it's only now, I hope you can feel them, Sopro."

Again, Sopro shimmered in response.

Woo Young
"Let everyone be able to love and be loved by the people they cherish. So that you, too, can be embraced by that love and find happiness."

As if the mother bird's heart was finally understood by its young child, Sopro gentry floated into the air. Fine cracks began to form over its surface, and at the height of its ascent, Sopro burst like fireworks creating a beautiful aurora as it scattered all over the world.











Woo Young

Woo Young

When Sopro painted a dazzling aurora across the sky, Yeo Sang pulled Woo Young into an embrace and sincirely apologized for his past behavior. The habits built up between them over a long time wouldn't disappear easily, but still, they understood each other better now that they had finally expressed the affection they had kept bottled up. Yeo Sang cleared his throat in embarrassment, and Woo Young burst out laughing at his reation. The laughing quicky spread, and soon a smile appeared on Yeo Sang's face as well.

San

This time, it was San who chimed in and spoke to Woo Young. When Woo Young asked what it was, San replied:

It was the slogan written on San's food truck. A phrase shared with him by the owner of a street food stall who had shown kidness to a wandering San nearly three years ago.

San

When Woo Young looked at San with a puzzled expression, San splashed the remaining water from a nearby flower bed at Woo Young and spoke. Woo Young groaned that now San was starting to has him since Yeo Sang had stopped, and everyone burst out laughing.

San

A long cleaning session followed. While it would take a bit more time for the disarrayed village to be fully restored, it was now in a much better state than before. As they spent the long hours cleaning, each of the members took time to reflect on their own hearts. And the realized: 'My emotions belong to me, so I shouldn't blame others for them. I need to accept and embrace them instead.'

The passing day slipped away before they knew it, and another day began. Sitting side by side on top of the food truck, the members too a brief rest while looking out at the village, now carrying the promise of a new beginning.

Jong Ho

Even if no one watches it, someday, after a very long time has passed, when we've all become grandfathers, we can pull out music we left on that channel and listen to it again. Wouldn't that bring back the memories of these times? Just like looking through old photographs."

Jong Ho
"Right. It's living our lives with just a single drop of a dream spilled into our everyday routines. And what matters more than that is the fact that we're doing it together."

With a chorus of "Sounds good", "I like it", and " That's a great idea", all the members agreed with Hong Ho's suggestion. Even while living a life beyond their dreams, they could still continue to dream. It didn't have to be anything grand. If only they could drop a single drop of a dream into their everyday lives, that wouls be enough.
